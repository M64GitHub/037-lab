Melody Lab

Generates melodies from the current scale on a 16th-note grid, tracker-style. Click a note to reroll it · double-click to mute it to a rest · Ctrl+Z undoes. ··· = rest, | = note still ringing · 3xx = slide into the note (tone portamento) · the Arp column is the chord channel: one note per bar plus Jxy, the tracker arpeggio command with the chord’s offsets (Voicing picks the inversion) · the Bass column shows the octave-bass figure playing underneath · the Drums column is a chip noise kit (KIK SNR HAT OHH) that exports as instrument numbers 01–04. The IT / SWM buttons in the column headers copy that column to the clipboard for DUET, OpenMPT or Schism.
